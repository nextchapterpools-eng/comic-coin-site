Why It Matters

Trust shouldn't end at the slab.

🤝

You always keep your comics

We never ask any collector or shop to ship us a comic or hand it over for custody. No vaulting, no shipping, no middleman holding your book — it stays exactly where it belongs, with you.

📊

Graded to industry standards

Condition is assessed against the same 0.5–10.0 scale used by leading grading companies, backed by real photos saved permanently to the ledger — not just a claim on a piece of paper.

🔎

Verify in seconds

Scan the QR code on any Comic Coin book and see its full, tamper-evident record instantly — no accounts, no guesswork.

🏪

Protect your shop

Give customers a reason to trust your inventory — and make it harder for stolen or misrepresented books to move quietly.

How It Works

From intake to verified sale.

1

Shop Intake

A participating shop photographs the comic and logs its condition.

2

Minted On-Chain

A verified record is created on the XRP Ledger, tied to that exact copy.

3

QR Sticker Applied

The book gets a scannable link to its permanent, verifiable record.

4

Verified Every Time

Every future sale or recheck updates the same record — a real history, not a one-time snapshot.

Simple, Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees. No surprises.

Monthly shop subscription

For Collectors

Not just for shops — your own collection too.

You don't need a shop to use Comic Coin. Any collector can create their own account, mint a verified record for a comic they own, and build a real, provable history for their personal collection — the same trust and provenance a shop gets, for your own books.
